NSW Camping Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

NSW Camping respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the NSW Camping mobile application.

Information We Collect

NSW Camping does not require users to create an account or provide their name, address, phone number or other personal identification information to use the main features of the app.

Some app features may require access to information or services on your device, such as location services or internet access.

Location Information

If you choose to allow location permission, NSW Camping may use your device's location to provide location-based features such as displaying your position relative to campgrounds, camping locations, dump points and other places shown within the app.

Location access is only used for app functionality and is not sold to third parties.

Maps and Third-Party Services

NSW Camping may use third-party services, including Google Maps and related Google services, to provide mapping, directions and location functionality. These services may process certain device or usage information according to their own privacy policies.

Internet Access

The app may access the internet to load maps, directions, external information or other online services required by particular features.

Saved Camps and Preferences

Information such as saved camping locations and app preferences may be stored locally on your device to provide app functionality.

External Links

NSW Camping may contain links to external websites or services, including campground information, booking services, directions and other resources. NSW Camping is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites or services.

Data Sharing

NSW Camping does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by third-party services where necessary to provide specific app functionality.

Children's Privacy

NSW Camping is a general camping and travel information application and is not designed specifically to collect personal information from children.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information associated with the app. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features, services or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the NSW Camping app developer through the contact details provided on the app's Google Play Store listing.
