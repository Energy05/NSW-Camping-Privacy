CampBase NSW Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

CampBase NSW is a camping, travel and community app. This policy explains what information the app handles, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information we handle

  • Account information: email address, authentication identifier and camper display name when you create a Camp Chat account.
  • Community content: Camp Chat posts, replies, private messages, uploaded photos and reports you submit.
  • Location: your device location when you grant permission, used for maps, nearby places, navigation and other location-based features.
  • Audio: microphone input only when you use bird identification. Audio is analysed for that feature and is not recorded continuously.
  • App information: saved camps, trip information, checklists, settings and other preferences, much of which is stored locally on your device.
  • Technical information: device, diagnostics, security and usage information that service providers may process to operate the app.

How information is used

Information is used to provide accounts and Camp Chat, show maps and nearby places, support navigation and notifications, remember preferences, respond to reports and support requests, prevent abuse, maintain security, and improve reliability.

Services and sharing

CampBase NSW does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by providers needed to operate app features, including Google Firebase services for authentication, cloud data and storage; Google and map providers for maps and directions; and notification or diagnostic services. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect users, the service or the public.

Content posted publicly in Camp Chat can be seen by other users. Private messages are visible to their conversation participants and may be accessed when reasonably necessary for safety, moderation, support or legal compliance.

Permissions and choices

You can decline or revoke optional permissions such as location, microphone, photos/camera and notifications in Android settings. Some related features will then be unavailable. Camp Chat users can report content and block other campers. Blocking hides that camper's content on the device.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains on your device until you remove it, clear app data or uninstall the app. Account and community information is kept while needed to provide the service, maintain safety, resolve reports and meet legal obligations. You can request permanent deletion in the app or through the account deletion page. Requests are normally processed within 30 days. Limited records may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or legal compliance.

Age requirement

Camp Chat is for people aged 16 or older. The wider app provides general camping information and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Security and external links

Reasonable safeguards are used, but no electronic service can guarantee absolute security. The app links to third-party websites such as campground, booking, map and social pages; their own privacy practices apply.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app features, services or legal requirements change. The date above will be revised when changes are published.
