CampBase NSW Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

CampBase NSW is a camping, travel and community app. This policy explains what information the app handles, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information we handle

  • Account information: email address, authentication identifier and camper display name when you create a Camp Chat account.
  • Community content: Camp Chat posts, replies, private messages, campsite ratings and comments, camper photos, campsite submissions, community events and reports you submit.
  • Location: your device location when you grant permission, used for maps, nearby places, navigation and other location-based features.
  • Audio: microphone input when you choose a voice feature, such as speaking a question or identifying a bird. The app does not listen continuously. Spoken assistant questions use the phone’s speech-recognition service; its provider may process audio under its own settings and policy. The recognised text is sent as an assistant question.
  • App information: saved camps, trip information, checklists, appearance settings, optional read-aloud preference and other preferences. Some data is kept on your device. Features that offer account-based cloud syncing use your signed-in Firebase account.
  • Technical information: device, diagnostics, security and usage information that service providers may process to operate the app.

How information is used

Information is used to provide accounts and Camp Chat, show maps and nearby places, support navigation and notifications, remember preferences, respond to reports and support requests, prevent abuse, maintain security, and improve reliability.

Services and sharing

CampBase NSW does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by providers needed to operate app features, including Google Firebase services for authentication, cloud data and storage; Google and map providers for maps, nearby places and directions; OpenAI for assistant responses; the phone’s chosen speech services for recognition and read-aloud; and notification or diagnostic services. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect users, the service or the public.

Content posted publicly in Camp Chat can be seen by other users. Private messages are visible to their conversation participants and may be accessed when reasonably necessary for safety, moderation, support or legal compliance.

Search or ask CampBase and OpenAI

Typed questions and text recognised from spoken questions are processed through CampBase’s authenticated backend using the OpenAI API. The request includes the question, recent assistant conversation, selected filter or campsite context and relevant catalogue records. Do not include passwords, payment details or information you do not want processed by these services.

Device location is used by the app’s nearby-place, map, fuel and routing tools when permission is granted. A town or location you type in a question forms part of that question. Relevant location and route information may be processed by map, places and directions providers to provide the requested results.

CampBase disables optional stored response history in its OpenAI requests. This does not guarantee zero provider retention: OpenAI may retain operational or abuse-monitoring data under its applicable policies. See OpenAI API data controls.

Optional read-aloud

Read aloud and Read answers aloud use your phone’s text-to-speech service. The app prefers an available offline Australian English voice. Other installed voices may use their provider’s network service and privacy settings. You can stop playback, turn off automatic reading, or change the phone’s voice settings. Playback stops when the assistant leaves the foreground.

Booking, fuel and external sources

Booking buttons open recorded booking or source links where available. Reservations and payments are completed with the external provider, whose terms and privacy practices apply. The app does not turn an AI response into a confirmed booking. Fuel prices come from the station-reported NSW FuelCheck feed and may be cached while refreshing; confirm the pump price before purchasing.

Permissions and choices

You can decline or revoke optional permissions such as location, microphone, photos/camera and notifications in Android settings. Some related features will then be unavailable. Read-aloud and dark mode can be controlled in the app; speech voices and recognition settings are controlled by your phone. Camp Chat users can report content and block other campers. Blocking hides that camper's content on the device.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains on your device until you remove it, clear app data or uninstall the app. Account and community information is kept while needed to provide the service, maintain safety, resolve reports and meet legal obligations. You can permanently delete your account and associated data yourself from the Camp Chat account panel. Deletion starts immediately after password confirmation and does not require contacting us. See the account deletion page for instructions. Limited records may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or legal compliance.

Age requirement

Camp Chat is for people aged 16 or older. The wider app provides general camping information and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Security and external links

Reasonable safeguards are used, but no electronic service can guarantee absolute security. The app links to third-party websites such as campground, booking, map and social pages; their own privacy practices apply.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app features, services or legal requirements change. The date above will be revised when changes are published.
