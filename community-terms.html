Camp Chat Community Terms

Last updated: 9 September 2026

By creating or using a Camp Chat account, you agree to these terms. Camp Chat is for people aged 16 or older.

Be safe and respectful

  • Do not harass, threaten, bully, impersonate or discriminate against others.
  • Do not post illegal, hateful, sexually explicit, violent or dangerous content.
  • Do not share another person's private information without permission.
  • Do not request or post passwords, bank details or other sensitive financial information.
  • Do not spam, scam, mislead users, advertise repeatedly or manipulate engagement.
  • Only upload content you have the right to share.
  • Do not use Camp Chat to arrange unsafe or unlawful activity.

Camping information

Community posts may be incomplete, outdated or incorrect. Check official land-manager notices, closures, fire restrictions, weather, road conditions and booking requirements before travelling. You remain responsible for your own safety and decisions.

Reports, blocking and moderation

You can report posts, replies and messages and block other campers using the menus in Camp Chat. Blocking hides that camper's content on your device. Reports may be reviewed for safety and policy enforcement. We may remove content, limit features, suspend or delete accounts, and preserve relevant records when reasonably necessary to protect users or comply with law.

Your account

Keep your sign-in details secure and provide accurate account information. You are responsible for activity through your account. You may request account deletion using the account deletion page.

Changes and contact

These terms may change as the service develops. Continued use after updated terms are published means you accept the revised terms.
