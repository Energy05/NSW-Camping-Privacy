Delete your CampBase NSW account

You can permanently delete your CampBase NSW account and associated data yourself from inside the app. You do not need to contact us.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Camp Chat.
  2. Open the account panel using the settings icon.
  3. Select Delete Account & Data.
  4. Enter your password and select Delete Permanently.

Deletion starts immediately after your identity is confirmed. This action cannot be undone.

What is deleted

The deletion process removes your authentication account and associated data under our control, including your Camp Chat profile, posts, replies, private conversations, uploaded photos, reports, reviews, saved camps, notification tokens and other account-linked contributions.

If you cannot access the app

Email for deletion help

Contact us from the email address connected to your account where possible. Do not send your password.

Limited retention

Limited records may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or legal compliance. Data held in service-provider backups may remain until the normal backup cycle expires.
