Delete your CampBase NSW account

You can request permanent deletion of your CampBase NSW account and associated Camp Chat data.

Request deletion in the app

  1. Open Camp Chat.
  2. Open the account panel.
  3. Select Delete Account & Data.
  4. Confirm and send the pre-filled email request.

Request deletion on the web

Email a deletion request

Send the request from the email address connected to the account. If that is not possible, include enough information for us to verify ownership safely. Do not send your password.

What is deleted

After verification, the account, authentication record and associated Camp Chat data under our control are scheduled for permanent deletion. This can include the display name, posts, replies, private messages, uploaded media and reports associated with the account.

Timing and limited retention

Requests are normally processed within 30 days. Limited records may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or legal compliance. Data held in service-provider backups may remain until the normal backup cycle expires.
